# Metamodel graph views

Three graph views of the metamodel, **generated by
`tools/generate-metamodel-views.py`** from the entity specifications and the
ontology.

| View | Source | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| [01 — entity graph](01-entity-graph.md) | `entities/*.md` relationship tables | what the Markdown says relates to what |
| [02 — class hierarchy](02-class-hierarchy.md) | `ontology/*.ttl` `rdfs:subClassOf` | the abstraction structure |
| [03 — relationship graph](03-relationship-graph.md) | `ontology/*.ttl` object properties | which classes the declared properties actually connect |

**These are the first mechanically generated projections in the repository.**
Every other index and register is maintained by hand, which is the debt
`ISSUE-0037` records. These are regenerated by running one command.

## How they are meant to be used

**Inspect the graphs, not the specifications.** Structures that are invisible
across twenty-two separate documents — near-identical neighbourhoods, isolated
nodes, hubs, pass-through chains, repeated motifs — are obvious in a graph, and
each view computes those metrics rather than leaving them to the eye.

The simplification review (`ISSUE-0074`) is performed against these views.

## What the graphs showed

Recorded from the metrics alone. Nothing below came from re-reading a
specification.

### 1. `Dimension` is a pass-through, confirmed three ways

The entity graph lists `DimensionSpecification` as both **pendant** (degree 1)
and a **chain node** (in-degree 1, out-degree 1). The relationship graph lists
`Dimension` as a chain node independently.

The path is:

```text
DimensionAssignment --along--> Dimension --specified-by--> DimensionSpecification
```

**A two-hop path in which the middle node adds nothing.** This is what an
unnecessary chain looks like when it is drawn rather than described, and it is
the third independent confirmation of `FINDINGS.md` #2.

### 2. `StateMachine` is pendant in every view that contains it

Degree 1 in the entity graph, degree 1 in the relationship graph. Its single edge
is `specified-by`.

**A node whose only edge is to the thing that defines it is not a node.**

### 3. The metamodel has almost no reusable relationship vocabulary

The relationship graph reports **no relation used three or more times** across 28
properties and 21 classes. Every relationship is bespoke.

For an architecture whose stated design unit is the relationship (`ADR-0067`),
this is the most consequential thing the graphs showed. **The relationship
vocabulary has never been factored.** `RelationshipType` exists to hold that
vocabulary and the vocabulary does not yet exist.

### 4. `governs` names three different relationships

The entity graph reports `governs` used three times; the relationship graph
reports it used once. The discrepancy is the finding.

The ontology had to rename one occurrence to `governsType` to avoid a clash —
and a rename forced by a clash is the signature of a term covering more than one
thing:

| Occurrence | Actually means |
|---|---|
| `Policy.governs` | constrains normatively |
| `StateMachineSpecification.governs` | controls the lifecycle of |
| `Dimension.governs` | may classify |

**This is a candidate tenth terminology split** (`ADR-0057`), and the first found
by comparing two generated views rather than by writing a specification.

### 5. `scoped-to` ×4 is `ContextualEntity`, rediscovered mechanically

The entity graph's top repeated motif is `scoped-to`, used four times. That is
exactly the repetition `FINDINGS.md` #3 identified by hand when reading the
specifications as OWL.

**The graph found it without anyone reading anything** — which is the argument
for having built these views.

### 6. The class hierarchy is two levels deep and has one intermediate abstraction

Every leaf in view 02 is pendant; the only non-trivial nodes are
`DescriptiveEntity`, `OperationalEntity` and `ContextualEntity`.

The Jaccard metric returns 1.0 for fifteen pairs, which is **uninformative on a
depth-2 tree** — every leaf shares its only parent. That is a fact about the
hierarchy, not about the pairs: **there is almost no intermediate abstraction**,
and the one that exists was discovered by the OWL checkpoint rather than
authored.

### 7. `Artifact` is the dominant hub, and it is descriptive

Degree 9 in the entity graph and 5 in the relationship graph — the highest in
both, and reached by operational entities constantly (`ADR.corrects`,
`Policy.governs`, `Skill.produces`, `Invariant.enforced-at`).

**This is where the two families actually touch.** `ADR-0065` separated them and
did not say where they meet; the graph answers that they meet at `Artifact`.

### 8. `EngineeringGate` and `Policy` have 0.67-similar neighbourhoods

The only near-identical pair in the entity graph, sharing `Artifact` and
`Workflow`. Both constrain engineering activity — one normatively, one by
review.

**Not proposed as a merge.** Recorded because it is the only structural
similarity the entity graph found, and the review should say explicitly why it is
not one.

### 9. `EvidenceKind` is isolated in the class hierarchy

Degree 0. It is a class in the ontology that is not a `MetamodelEntity`, because
it is a vocabulary rather than an entity.

Correct, and it flags something real: **once `Vocabulary` is specified,
`EvidenceKind` should be an instance of it rather than a bare class.** The same
will apply to issue status, acceptance decision and gate outcomes.

## Status

```sh
python3 tools/generate-metamodel-views.py
```
