## What was learned

### The plan surfaced the model's own gap as an unmet precondition

Planning a change to `conflict.go` leaves one completion condition unchecked:
*the constraints on this artifact are known and were reviewed*.

That is the traceability gap found in `SESSION-0033` — nothing constrains
`Concept.Conflict` — **arriving unprompted as a reason the work is not ready to
start.** The same plan against `Artifact.MetaV1Types` checks that box.

Last session a query found the gap. This session a plan **used** it: the same
fact stopped being an observation and became a precondition. That is the
difference between knowledge and guidance, demonstrated rather than argued.

### The proposal rejects both options it was offered

`EngineeringIntent` **passes all three of the project's admission tests** —
`ADR-0067`, `ADR-0076`, `ADR-0065`. On existing criteria it is admissible as a
Layer A operational entity.

The criterion that decides it **is not yet written**:

> **A Layer A entity is one whose instances belong in a model. An intent belongs
> to a session.**

Every existing Layer A entity has instances that persist as facts about a system.
No repository would contain `Intent.AddFeature` as a node describing itself. It
is the same kind of thing as a query's `subject`.

And it is not a Recommendation specialization either: a recommendation's `intent`
field describes *the recommendation*, not the developer's goal —
`R-change-concept` and `R-change-implementation` are both *Modify Behavior*.
Merging them would mean **one intent per recommendation**, inverting a
relationship where an intent should select many.

**Proposed: a registry beside them.** It buys the one genuinely new relationship
— *intent selects plans and recommendations* — costs no metamodel change, and is
**reversible**. Promoting a registry to an entity is mechanical; demoting an
entity is not.

### The measure is honest and gameable, and both were recorded

`ADR-0093` reports 10 derived and 3 deferred for the `conflict.go` plan. It is
also **inflatable by listing trivially derived facts**, and some decisions should
never be derived — a plan that eventually defers nothing would be a plan that has
started guessing.

Recording both in the ADR was more useful than defending the metric.

### Four of nine loop steps exist

Naming the destination made the distance measurable. **Reasoning, Plan,
Recommendations and the model exist.** Goal, Execution Plan, Task Graph, AI
workers, Verification and Knowledge Update do not — **and the loop does not
close**, which is the step that would turn this from a tool into a system that
learns.
