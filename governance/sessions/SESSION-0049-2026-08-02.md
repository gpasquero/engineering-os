## What was learned

### A prediction was made and it was wrong

`ADR-0130` stated: *the fix should add zero knowledge and move understanding.*

**Knowledge went from 94 to 103 curated sources.** Three of the four restored
relationships add nothing — they attach existing entities. The fourth, `C4`,
adds `Artifact` nodes, because **there was no route rule in Continuous
Acquisition at all**: a controller added after onboarding never entered the
model, so there was nothing for its capability to be implemented by.

The prediction was right about the mechanism and wrong about the scope, and it
was falsifiable enough to catch that. **The general claim survives — the fix
moved understanding — and the specific one did not.**

### One of the four restored predicates produces nothing, and it does not matter

`scoped-to` emits **zero edges** in the frozen suite. The curation policy never
accepts the `BoundedContext`, so the relationship has nothing to point at.

Judged as *semantic parity*, the work is 3 of 4 and something is unfinished.
Judged as `ADR-0134`'s contract — **`EQ-06` answers and retention is 100 % — the
work is done**, and `scoped-to` is a detail that happens not to matter in this
repository under this curation.

**Only one of those two readings tells a customer something true**, and the
reframing arrived in the same session that produced the case for it.

### The suite is now the only thing that can say a change worked

Nothing else moved. The test suite passed before and after. The governance corpus
was consistent before and after. No diagnostic fired.

**Seventeen fixtures, twenty registries, two query engines and 300 records were
all equally happy with a model in which nine of ten capabilities were isolated.**

The frozen suite is the only instrument that distinguished the two states, which
is what `ADR-0129` meant by *the benchmark owns part of the architecture*.

### Maintenance got more expensive, and that is the correct trade

278 → 314 proposals, **13 % → 15 %** of re-running discovery every time.

The cheapest possible maintenance preserves nothing. **Cost is telemetry and it
fails the metric admission test** (`ADR-0132`) — a preservation fix that made
maintenance free would be a regression wearing a good number.

### The North Star moved past the work in the same session that delivered it

Retention is 100 %. Under `ADR-0131` that is success; under `ADR-0136` it is **an
unfinished argument**, because nothing yet shows a team decides better because
`EQ-06` answers.

**A North Star the current work already satisfies is a description, not a
direction.**
