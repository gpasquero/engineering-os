# SESSION-0021 — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Record the owner's acceptance of `SESSION-0020` and the two decisions closing
its open question — then, mid-session, act on a change of advancement criterion:
**architecture through implementation.**

## Acceptance recorded

`Active`.

## Decisions made

**`ADR-0060` — Mechanical Discovery is compilation; Interpretive Discovery is
authoring.** The word "discovery" conflated two activities. Traceability,
dependency graphs, registry projections and consistency checks are derivable by
deterministic algorithm and belong to the compiler. Architectural patterns,
recurring design principles and semantic clusters require judgment, so they are
**authoring**: their output is a proposal entering the normal acceptance
workflow. **No principle required modification.** Resolves `ISSUE-0071`.

**`ADR-0061` — four categories of knowledge.** Authored, Mechanical,
Interpretive, Operational. **The Knowledge Compiler is not an intelligence. It
is a deterministic semantic compiler. Intelligence remains outside it and
participates only through Authoring.**

**`ADR-0062` — architecture through implementation.** The order of priority
changes. If an existing decision permits building, build. Stop only for a real
contradiction. Avoid new architectural concepts unless strictly necessary for
the next step.

## The pivot

Twenty sessions produced **62 decisions, 73 issues, 16 acceptance records — and
no artifact outside `governance/`.** Every session answered the previous
session's questions and opened roughly two more.

The criterion is now: *"Do we need to resolve this in order to build the next
deliverable?"* A negative answer produces architectural debt.

### What was done about it

**22 open issues were re-triaged as `deferred` architectural debt.** Two remain
open, and neither is architectural: `ISSUE-0011` (public repository, no licence
— a legal exposure) and `ISSUE-0037` (hand-maintained projections — operational
debt that B5 discharges).

**The roadmap was restructured** around six build deliverables cutting a

**`model/metamodel/` was created.** The first artifact outside `governance/` in
twenty-one sessions: a README, a complete inventory of 25 entities with the

`ArtifactRevision` and `DimensionSpecification`.

## What was learned

**Building immediately produced findings that analysis had not.**

- **`ArtifactRevision` had to say "undefined"** for its own identity property.
  `ISSUE-0007` had been open for nineteen sessions as an abstract question about
  versioning granularity. Writing one entity turned it into a blank field.
- **`ArtifactRevision` was written before `Artifact`**, which it references
  throughout and which does not exist. The wrong order, discovered by writing
  rather than by planning.
- **Three entities in `ADR-0035`'s inventory have never been defined anywhere** —
  Capability, Concept, Validation. `SESSION-0017` recorded this as a prediction;
  the inventory makes it a visible gap in a table.
- **Four entities in that inventory belong to the compiler architecture**, which
  `ADR-0053` says the metamodel must not contain. Recorded in the inventory as
  debt rather than resolved, per the new criterion.

Four findings from writing two specifications. That is the argument for the
pivot, produced by the pivot.

**`ADR-0060` adopted the reading `ISSUE-0071` had recorded** — the second time
an issue's own suggested answer was taken, after `ISSUE-0045`. Both times the
issue had reasoned to the answer and stopped short of asserting it.

**`ADR-0061` states what "AI-first" means here**, which the project had never
pinned down. Intelligence is central and participates *as an author*, under the
same acceptance discipline as a human. It is not inside the transformation
layer.

**The new criterion was applied to this session's own output.** `ISSUE-0072` and
`ISSUE-0073` were opened as blockers under the old criterion and deferred as
debt under the new one within the same session.
