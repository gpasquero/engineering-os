## What was learned

### Contamination suppressed the earlier result rather than inflating it

The blind worker produced **20 proposals and 11 distribution claims**; the
contaminated one produced **4 and 2**.

The contaminated worker had constrained itself to avoid ground the deterministic
rules covered. **The blind worker, given a contract and no such knowledge,
investigated the whole model.**

That inverts the expected direction of the bias, and it is the finding about
method: **blindness mattered, and not in the way anticipated.**

### Complementarity is total

**13 of 13 blind invariants have no deterministic counterpart.** Nearest word
overlap never exceeds 0.31.

They are different kinds of statement. `R4` transcribes what a test asserts —
`RejectsA7CharPasswordBelowMinimum`. The blind worker states what must be true of
the system — `SecretsAreNeverRecoverableFromStorage`, synthesized across three
credential types in three tables.

**Neither is a better version of the other**, and the question the benchmark
existed to answer is answered.

### A blind worker found a real bug in the extractor that produced its input

> *"the extractor appears to attribute every column in a file to every table
> declared in it"*

Correct. `csat-surveys.ts` declares two tables and the extractor gave both the
same fifteen columns. **Fixed** — 10 and 8 — and the vocabulary version moved to
`1.1.0`.

**It read a 137 KB JSON file and nothing else.** Four of its seven gaps concern
the Mechanical Model rather than the repository: `F-fact-absent` reported by the
interpreter, unprompted, which is exactly what `ADR-0110`'s taxonomy was written
to make possible and what no deterministic rule can do about its own input.

### The worker found more contract defects than the author had

Six, and two could not be fixed by editing the contract:

**Type enforcement.** A skill declares `proposal-types` and **nothing checks that
a worker honoured it** — the worker caught its own violation on self-check and
reported that nothing else would have.

**Stopping conditions against proposal counts.** *Every module placed and every
route family characterised* forced 28 modules and 26 families; the requested
8–15 was impossible. **A genuine design conflict**, now stated rather than
hidden.

The other four are fixed: the missing `specializes` field, undefined
*"placed"* and *"characterised"*, a question no permitted evidence could answer,
and the extraction artefact.

**A contract that survives its first execution unchanged has not been tested.**

### The comparison harness refused on its first run

Adding `repository` and `vocabularyVersion` to the Mechanical Model changed its
digest, and `tools/compare-interpreters.py` **refused to compare** the
deterministic re-run against probabilistic workers that had seen the previous
model.

That is `ADR-0108` enforced: **two interpreters that saw different evidence are
not comparable.** It also exposed a design defect in the harness itself — it was
re-extracting rather than loading the frozen input, so it was comparing against a
model no probabilistic worker had seen. **Fixed by pinning the comparison to the
frozen file**, which is what *frozen* was supposed to mean.

## Recommended next action

**Interpreter experimentation stops.** The question is answered — 13 of 13, zero
overlap — and further comparison refines a number nobody is waiting on.

**The acquisition lifecycle.** Initial Acquisition using both worker classes,
authorize a substantial coherent model, compile and generate the navigable
product, run the Director, make one bounded change, maintain through Continuous
Acquisition, run Periodic Reacquisition, produce the first Knowledge Drift
Report.

**The step that will hurt is Continuous Acquisition**, and it is the one that
matters: a drift report against a model nothing has maintained measures
onboarding, not maintenance. **Everything before it is a static demonstration.**

**One caution.** Two findings this session came from a worker reading its own
input critically — the extractor bug and four `F-fact-absent` gaps. **Nothing in
the architecture asks a worker to do that**; it was volunteered. A Skill that
required it would probably be worth writing.
