# SESSION-0010 — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Record the owner's acceptance of `SESSION-0009`, resolve the policy-naming
collision, and name the architectural pattern the project had rediscovered four
times.

## Acceptance recorded

**`ACCEPT-0005`** — the Project Owner accepted the `SESSION-0009` decisions at
revision `7af8f44`. Those revisions are now `Active`.

The rationale names the direction: convergence toward **a cleaner separation
between historical knowledge and operational knowledge**.

## Decisions made

**`ADR-0030` — a taxonomy for normative artifacts.** Three distinct artifact
kinds: `GovernancePolicy` (rules governing Engineering OS itself),
`ModelingPolicy` (rules governing how domains must be modeled), `ProcessPolicy`
(rules governing execution of workflows). The unqualified term "Policy" is
avoided in specifications. **A normative artifact type name is always qualified
by what it governs** — the general naming rule. Resolves `ISSUE-0050`.

**`ADR-0031` — the Registry Pattern.** A Registry is an authoritative index of
semantic entities and never contains the complete specification; it references
independently versioned specifications. The registry answers what exists, where
it lives, relationships, ownership, status and version; the specification answers
complete semantics, constraints, examples, rationale and evolution. Every
extensible concept is evaluated for Registry + Specification modeling.

## What was learned

**A prediction recorded two sessions ago came true and was acted on.**
`ADR-0028` had written: *"Three independent arrivals at the same shape is close
to a principle. It should be named as one when the fourth appears."* The fourth
appeared — policies — and the pattern was named.

Worth noting as a property of the method, not just an anecdote: the observation
survived two sessions because it was written into an ADR rather than left in
conversation. That is the whole thesis of `ADR-0001` demonstrated on a small
scale.

**`ADR-0030` closes a class of defect rather than an instance, and it is the
second such closure.** `ADR-0025` fixed *state* names by namespacing to the
owning machine. `ADR-0030` fixes *normative artifact type* names by qualifying
with what they govern. Two dimensions, one discipline. The instruction was
explicit that ad-hoc prefixes were not the answer — which is what the project
had done three times before.

**The taxonomy resolved a hidden invariant leak.** `ADR-0023` says a governance
policy cannot modify itself and that the `Active` one governs its successor's
acceptance. Read against an unqualified "Policy", that invariant appeared to
constrain modeling rules too, meaning every modeling change would need two
acceptance cycles. Scoping it to `GovernancePolicy` removes a constraint nobody
intended.

**Naming the Registry Pattern immediately exposed a contradiction it inherits.**
`ADR-0031` calls a Registry an *authoritative* index. `ADR-0016` had made
indexes *projections*, generated from authoritative sources. `ADR-0012` said
manifests should be validated or partially generated. Three `Active` ADRs, three
different answers, and all three manifests are M2 deliverables.

A reconciliation probably works — a Registry holds facts *no specification
holds*, so it does not merely restate — but it is not what any of the three ADRs
says. Recorded as `ISSUE-0053` and marked `blocking`, because it cannot be
deferred past the first manifest. **This is the first issue in the project to
block a named M2 deliverable rather than the milestone.**

**A new named concept arrived without a definition.** The Knowledge Explorer is
named in the instruction as a future surface exposing registries, and it does not
appear in `ADR-0014`'s consumer list. It now carries a requirement with no
definition — `ISSUE-0052`.

**`ProcessPolicy`'s examples are the workflow catalogue's names.** Feature
implementation, bug investigation, release, migration — against M8's `feature`,
`bug`, `behavior-change`, `refactoring`. Whether a `ProcessPolicy` governs a
workflow or *is* its normative half is undefined, and the two are written in
different milestones. `ISSUE-0051`.
