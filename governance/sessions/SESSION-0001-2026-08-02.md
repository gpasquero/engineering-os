# SESSION-0001 — 2026-08-02

Read the inherited repository in full, then deliver M1: the repository
architecture and the documentation system that lets future sessions continue
without conversation history.

Explicitly out of scope: skills, policies, contracts.

## What was learned

**The inherited documents described two different trees under one name.** The
seven-directory "repository structure" in the design notes and the
fifteen-directory `model/` tree in the reconstruction prototype are the
methodology and its output respectively. This conflation was the root cause of
at least three downstream defects (`ISSUE-0014`, `ISSUE-0015`, and the
undifferentiated `shared/` in `ISSUE-0024`). Resolved by `ADR-0006`.

**The prototypes violate the principle they establish.** `DECISIONS.md`
mandates "shared policies instead of duplicated prompt text"; the twelve
assertion statuses are nonetheless defined twice inside the same prototype, and
two "minimum evidence records" disagree on their defaults. Recorded as
`ISSUE-0018` and `ISSUE-0019`, and the structural remedy is `ADR-0008`.

**`principal-engineering` is not a peer skill.** Despite describing itself in
its frontmatter as an "end-to-end engineering operating system", it is best read
as an early draft of the *workflow layer*. Its `Confidence Report` and
`Counterfactual Review` sections are cross-cutting quality gates with no home in
the planned skill catalogue. Recorded in `ISSUE-0017` and `ISSUE-0026`.

**Write scope is a safety property, not a style rule.** The reconstruction
prototype forbids touching production code; the change prototypes write it.
Composing them requires scope to change mid-run, which nothing defines. Raised
as a `risk`, not merely an inconsistency — `ISSUE-0021`.

**"Skill" carried two incompatible meanings**, which made `BOOTSTRAP.md`'s
"this is NOT a collection of Claude skills" read as a contradiction of the
architecture rather than a constraint on it. Resolving it in the glossary, and
fixing the adapter boundary in `ADR-0007`, unblocked M2 and M3 from
`ISSUE-0001`.

**The inherited build state overstated progress.** "Skill decomposition" was
recorded as complete; it was ten names with no definitions. This is exactly the
memory defect the documentation system now exists to prevent — recorded as
`ISSUE-0025`.
