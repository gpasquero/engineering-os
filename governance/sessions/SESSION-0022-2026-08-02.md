## Decisions made

**`ADR-0063` — Apache-2.0.** The repository is publicly reusable. SPDX
`Apache-2.0`, chosen for the explicit patent grant given that the framework will
ship tooling and executable code. `LICENSE` holds the canonical text, fetched
from `apache.org` and verified: 11 358 bytes, all nine sections, appendix
present. Resolves `ISSUE-0011`.

**`ADR-0064` — artifact and revision identity.** An Artifact has a stable
logical identifier; an ArtifactRevision has an immutable revision identifier
scoped to its Artifact. Identity is the pair `(artifact-id, revision-id)`. The
revision identifier **must not require Git** and may be *mapped* to a commit,
digest or external identifier. **A Git commit SHA is provenance, not identity.**
Resolves `ISSUE-0007`.

## What was learned

**`ISSUE-0007` was deferred as debt and resolved one session later.** It had
been open since `SESSION-0001` as an abstract question about versioning
granularity. `SESSION-0021` deferred it under the new criterion. Writing
`ArtifactRevision` turned it into a blank field, and it was answered.

That is `ADR-0062` working exactly as intended: the deferral was correct, and it
was short because implementation surfaced the question in a form that could be
answered.

**`Validation` was the eighth term this project has had to split** — after
"skill", "authoritative", "state", "policy", "registry", "layer/level" and
"level/process". It conflated `ValidationRule` (semantic), `ValidationResult`
(compiler or operational) and `Validator` (tooling).

It is the **first caught before being specified rather than after**.
`ADR-0057`'s Naming Qualification did its work three sessions after being
written.

**Writing specifications surfaced four missing entities.** `BoundedContext`,
`Invariant`, `Actor` and `Evidence` are referenced by the specifications written
this session and appear nowhere in the inventory. All four are central in the
inherited prototypes.

`BoundedContext` is referenced by **two independent specifications** —
`Concept` and `Capability` — which is the signal that it belongs in the next
batch rather than in the debt register.

**Two specifications describe shapes with no instances.** `Dimension` has no
registered dimension; `DimensionAssignment` has no assignment. Naming that is
more useful than implying otherwise: the mechanism is defined and unexercised.

**A capability with no realisation is a legitimate state.** Writing `Capability`
forced the cardinality question, and the answer matters for the methodology's
purpose: a specified capability the system does not have is exactly what a
reconstruction should be able to say.
