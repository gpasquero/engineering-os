## Decisions made

**`ADR-0110` — three acquisition stages**, and the decision's centre:

> **The trust boundary remains review and acceptance, not determinism.**

Deterministic discovery is reproducible, cheap and auditable; probabilistic
discovery synthesizes across weakly structured evidence. **Both are used and
neither is authoritative.**

It also requires that an interpretive failure be **classified before it is called
one** — five classes, and `SESSION-0040`'s failure was `F-fact-ignored`.

**`ADR-0111` — granularity preservation.** Both levels, related by
`specializes`, **using existing constructs and no new entity.** A specific
guarantee is a *narrower* `Invariant`, not a different kind — so a `Guarantee`
entity would fail `ADR-0067`.

**`ADR-0112` — three acquisition modes and the Knowledge Drift Report.**
Reacquisition **validates and challenges** the maintained model and never
overwrites it.

## What was learned

### The benchmark separates volume from abstraction

One frozen Mechanical Model, four interpreters.

**`R4` proposes 302 assertions and zero syntheses. The probabilistic worker
proposes 4 assertions and 4 syntheses.**

Those are different axes, and treating them as one was the mistake behind
`SESSION-0039`'s ceiling claim.

### The strongest finding required comparing a fact to its population

**Exactly one route of 161 carries the tenant in its URL path.** Every other
derives it from the token.

The consequence: **tenant isolation has two enforcement paths**, and nothing in
the repository records that one is different.

Every fact was extracted. **No declared rule compares a fact against the
distribution of all other facts of its kind** — and a rule could be written for
this case, and not for the general case. That is `F-rule-insufficient`, and it is
the only failure class that is evidence for probabilistic interpretation.

**Three of four probabilistic findings are `F-rule-insufficient`.**
`SESSION-0040`'s was `F-fact-ignored`. **The taxonomy earned its place in one
session**: the same shape of evidence meant opposite things twice, and without
the classification both would have read as *determinism ran out*.

### The experiment is contaminated and says so

The probabilistic worker had already seen `R1`, `R3` and `R4` output.

**Mitigation is not a fix.** No proposal is drawn from test text; all rest on
route, table, dependency or document facts. **A genuinely blind comparison needs
a worker that has not seen this repository**, and that is recorded as the next
step rather than claimed as done.

### The worker corrected itself mid-run, and that is provenance

`Concept.ApiKeyAuthentication` began as *a second authentication path exists and
is untested*. Checking the mechanical model showed **two test suites exist**. The
assumption was false and the finding is weaker.

**The discarded hypothesis is recorded in the proposal.** A worker reporting only
its surviving conclusions would be less auditable, not more.

### Assignment, not a winner

| Task | Class |
|---|---|
| enumerate anything | mechanical extractor |
| name a structural fact | deterministic rule |
| abstract a test suite, both levels | deterministic rule `R4` |
| compare a fact to its population | **probabilistic** |
| synthesize a process from fifty weak documents | **probabilistic** |
| decide what is true | **neither** — curation |

**Rules win wherever structure carries the meaning.** The probabilistic worker
wins where meaning is distributed across evidence that individually says nothing.
