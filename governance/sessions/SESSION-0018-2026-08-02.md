# SESSION-0018 — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Record the owner's acceptance of `SESSION-0017`, replace the plan to evaluate
dimensions individually with a review process, unmix the modeling hierarchy, and
record the separation those two decisions imply.

## Acceptance recorded

**`ACCEPT-0013`** — the Project Owner accepted the `SESSION-0017` decisions at
revision `1345bfc`. Those revisions are now `Active`.

The rationale: the project continues to **replace isolated architectural
decisions with reusable metamodel patterns**, and the four-stage hierarchy
provides a common modeling vocabulary for future extensible concepts.

## Decisions made

**`ADR-0051` — Dimension Review.** Do not evaluate candidates one by one; define
the evaluation process first. Four outcomes: accepted as a Dimension, or
rejected and modelled as metadata, a relationship, or another metamodel entity.
The decision becomes an authoritative artifact. `ADR-0049`'s five criteria
become **mandatory**. Resolves `ISSUE-0065`.

**`ADR-0052` — two orthogonal hierarchies.** Supersedes `ADR-0050`.
`Definition → Instance → Assignment` is semantic; **Projection belongs to the
compilation model**. Registry Projections, the Knowledge Explorer, documentation
and search indexes are compilation products, not semantic concepts. Resolves
`ISSUE-0066`.

**`ADR-0053` — semantic architecture is separate from compiler architecture.**
The metamodel defines what exists; the compiler defines how it is transformed;
neither embeds the other's concepts. Every new concept answers: semantic,
compilation, or both?

## What was learned

**"Process before instances" was applied twice in two sessions and is becoming
the project's default.** `ISSUE-0062` asked for four definitions and got a
specification entity. `ISSUE-0065` asked for nine evaluations and got an
evaluation process. Both times the answer refused the enumeration and built the
mechanism.

The stated reason is worth keeping: nine ad-hoc evaluations would establish nine
precedents rather than one procedure, and the tenth candidate would restart the
argument.

**`ADR-0050` was superseded one session after acceptance.** `ACCEPT-0013`
accepted it while `ISSUE-0066` was already open against it — the shortest
acceptance-to-supersession interval in the project. That is not a failure of
review; the hierarchy was correct about what it described and incomplete about
its own boundary, and naming it is what made the boundary visible.

**`ADR-0053` did work immediately.** Two dimension candidates now look like
compilation concepts wearing semantic clothing:

- **Compilation Phase** classifies artifacts by when a compiler touches them,
  which puts a compiler concept in the metamodel.
- **Representation** enumerates Semantic, Authoring and Presentation — which
  `ADR-0052` has just placed in the compilation hierarchy.

Both enter their Dimension Reviews with a specific objection rather than a
general doubt. A principle that produces concrete objections within minutes of
being written is doing more than restating an intuition.

**The metamodel-first gate bound for the first time on something the project
needs.** Dimension Review is a new artifact type. `ADR-0035` requires positioning
in the metamodel first, and `ADR-0038` requires naming the metamodel entity it
instantiates. **The metamodel does not exist.** This cannot be waved through
without making the gate advisory. `ISSUE-0067`.

**Three gates now govern new concepts, and they overlap.** `ADR-0035` (position
in the metamodel), `ADR-0038` (four questions), `ADR-0053` (three questions).
Nothing states how they compose — and `ADR-0038`'s fourth question, *which
compiler phase consumes or produces it*, now asks a compilation question of every
concept, including purely semantic ones that `ADR-0053` says are entitled to have
no answer. `ISSUE-0068`.

**A term arrived for something already named twice.** `ADR-0052`'s "Authoritative
Semantic Model" denotes what `ADR-0037` calls Layers A and B and `ADR-0047`
calls the Authoring Representation. Three names for one thing is the pattern
that has bitten this project six times. Recorded in `ADR-0052`'s consequences
for the metamodel work to reconcile.
