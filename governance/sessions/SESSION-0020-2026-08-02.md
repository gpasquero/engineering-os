# SESSION-0020 — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Record the owner's acceptance of `SESSION-0019`, close the terminology-collision
class, settle what a Principle is, and record what the compiler is ultimately
for.

## Acceptance recorded

**`ACCEPT-0015`** — the Project Owner accepted the `SESSION-0019` decisions at
revision `4d1c8d0`. Those revisions are now `Active`.

The rationale names what `ADR-0055` achieved without stating it in those terms:
moving evaluation questions from artifacts to Gates removed a coupling between
*what a concept is* and *how it is reviewed*. The session recorded the
consequence as a cost; the reviewer records the decoupling as the point.

## Decisions made

**`ADR-0057` — Naming Qualification.** A concept's canonical name includes its
architectural dimension whenever ambiguity is possible. Not renaming —
qualification. Ten canonical names fixed. Resolves `ISSUE-0069`.

**`ADR-0058` — Principles are semantic entities, not artifacts.** They emerge
from accepted architectural knowledge, live in the semantic model rather than
the document taxonomy, and are **extracted by the Knowledge Compiler**. Resolves
`ISSUE-0070`.

**`ADR-0059` — authored versus discovered knowledge.** Engineering OS maximizes
discovered knowledge. The compiler's higher purpose is revealing architectural
knowledge that exists implicitly across many artifacts but was never written as
a single document.

## What was learned

**A class of defect closed after eight instances.** "skill", "authoritative",
"state", "policy", "registry", "layer/level", "level/process" — each solved
individually, five by splitting and two by qualifying. `ADR-0057` makes
qualification the default rather than the remedy.

`ADR-0046` predicted this three sessions ago: *three ADRs applying one
discipline is a rule, and it belongs in a policy rather than being rediscovered
a fourth time.* It was rediscovered twice more before becoming one.

**It also named a concept the project could not previously express.** **Business
Process** — the domain processes an adopting repository certainly has, which
Engineering OS had no way to distinguish from its own. That is qualification
producing vocabulary rather than only disambiguating it.

**`ISSUE-0070`'s three options all assumed a Principle is authored.** Sixth
reframing. The answer removes the trade-off entirely: the document taxonomy
stays small *and* principles become queryable, because a Principle is not a
document at all.

It also fixes something the issue never raised. **A principle emerging across
several ADRs becomes one entity.** The Registry Pattern spans `ADR-0027`,
`ADR-0028`, `ADR-0031` and `ADR-0032`; as a semantic entity it is one node
rather than four cross-references.

**`ADR-0059` states why the compiler is worth building**, and this project is
its own evidence. The Registry Pattern took six independent arrivals across nine
sessions before anyone wrote it down. The reframing pattern in `SESSION-0015`,
and the recurrence of `Definition → Instance → Assignment`, were each found by a
human holding the whole corpus in mind. A compiler could do that systematically.

**Which is exactly why the open question is sharp.** `ADR-0020` requires the
compiler to be deterministic and forbids a generator invoking an agent.
Traceability and dependency graphs follow declared links and are mechanical.
**Architectural patterns and semantic clusters are judgements** — the kind this
project's reviewer has been making for twenty sessions.

Either discovery is algorithmic, or the determinism rule needs qualifying.
`ISSUE-0071` records a reading that would preserve every rule: **deterministic
discovery is compilation; non-deterministic discovery is authoring**, so an
agent-proposed pattern becomes an authored artifact entering the normal
acceptance workflow. That preserves `ADR-0015` exactly — and places the most
ambitious discoveries outside the compiler.

**"Maximize" has no stated limit.** Over-discovery would put confident
falsehoods into the Canonical Knowledge Model with the authority of derivation,
and discovered knowledge is never accepted because acceptance applies to
authoritative artifacts. Recorded as a negative consequence rather than assumed
away.
