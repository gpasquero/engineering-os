## Decisions made

**`ADR-0123` — a continuously improving Engineering Understanding System**,
superseding `ADR-0122`. **Memory stores. Understanding explains. Guidance
recommends. Acquisition learns. Drift challenges.** Five verbs, and none alone
is the product. It also records the transition the project is approaching: *the
product is no longer acquisition; acquisition enables guidance, and guidance is
what customers buy.*

**`ADR-0124` — Discovery Skills have three maturity levels.** Level 1 is
repository-specific and **disposable**; only levels 2 and 3 are catalog assets.
This gives `ADR-0119` a third option between overfitting and doing nothing.

**`ADR-0125` — a repository is one evidence source.** The Engineering Model is
the stable representation. Runtime observations, incidents, issue trackers and
human reviews all enrich the same twenty-three entities.

**`ADR-0126` — questions have levels, and only `repository` is built.** The
deferral is the decision.

## What was learned

### The model grew 2.4× and answered one question *worse*

The flat total hides the only thing that moved. **`EQ-06` — *which capabilities
would be affected by this feature?* — was `answered` at `t0` and `partial` at
every step since.**

The model went from 39 proposals to 94 curated sources. **It answered the
planning question at its smallest and stopped answering it at its largest.**

### Continuous Acquisition adds nodes and does not connect them

```text
94 nodes · 36 edges · 0.38 edges per node
capabilities: 10        with no edge at all: 9
```

Rule `C3-new-module` proposes a `Capability` for a new module directory and
proposes **no relationship**. The equivalent initial rule,
`S1-module-is-a-capability`, emits `scoped-to`.

**The two acquisition modes disagree about the same evidence** — the same family
as the `C1`/`R4` divergence from `SESSION-0043`, and the second defect found by
comparing two modes rather than by checking either.

**Not fixed here, deliberately.** The remedy is a decision about *which
relationships Continuous Acquisition is entitled to infer from a mechanical
delta*, and that belongs in a stated decision rather than in a diff.

### A number this project published was measured at the flattest point

`SESSION-0043` reported Continuous Acquisition at **1.3 % of a rerun**, and it
has been in the build state ever since. It reproduces exactly — **at `t9`**, the
last commit of the history, where almost nothing changes.

Across the whole history the honest figure is **13 %**, ranging from **0 % to
59 %**. Early in a system's life, continuous maintenance costs more than half of
a full rediscovery.

> **A single-step measurement of an incremental process reports the step it was
> taken at, not the process.**

The 1.3 % was not wrong. It was published without its position in the history,
and that omission flattered the system.

### Drift grew, and the mechanism reported it

`D-unsupported-assertion` went from **1 to 3**, and
`D-knowledge-without-implementation: 9` appeared for the first time — nine
curated assertions whose implementation the repository no longer shows.

**This is the closest the project has come to exercising the retraction path**,
and it remains a report rather than a retraction, which is correct.

### The rephrasing was not cosmetic, and this run proves it

Under `ADR-0122` — *engineering memory* — this run is a **success**: nothing
lost, everything maintained, maintenance cheap.

Under `ADR-0123` — *a continuously improving Engineering Understanding System* —
it is a **failure**, because understanding is the thing that was supposed to
improve and it is flat.

**The experiment is the first thing in this project that could tell the two
apart**, and it was run in the same session that adopted the distinction.

### Four of five verbs are strong and the fifth has never been tried

Acquisition, Memory and Drift all work. **Understanding is at 22 % and 33 %.
Guidance has never run against a model it did not build for itself.**

That ordering is the reviewer's transition stated as a measurement.
