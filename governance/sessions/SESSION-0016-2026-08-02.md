# SESSION-0016 — 2026-08-02

## Objective

Record the owner's acceptance of `SESSION-0015`, resolve the three open
questions it raised, and record the principle that explains how they fit
together.

## Acceptance recorded

**`ACCEPT-0011`** — the Project Owner accepted the `SESSION-0015` decisions at
revision `12692f0`. Those revisions are now `Active`.

The rationale names the milestone more precisely than the session did: the
project has **separated the object model from the classification model**, which
is what allows the metamodel to stay stable while classification evolves.

## Decisions made

**`ADR-0044` — independence is not isolation.** Dimensions are semantically
independent; values are never derived from one another. But dimensions may
declare relationships describing compatibility, applicability or constraints —
**descriptive, never inferential**. No dimension derives another's value unless
an explicit Inference Rule exists, and **Inference Rules, if ever introduced,
are their own first-class artifact type**. Resolves `ISSUE-0059`.

**`ADR-0045` — front matter is interchange syntax.** An authoritative artifact
exposes a **Human Representation**: a canonical serialization of semantic
assignments, not the semantic source of truth. The compiler reconstructs
relationships from it. Resolves `ISSUE-0060`.

**`ADR-0046` — Abstraction Level and Semantic Layer.** Both concepts are valid
and describe different dimensions. Qualified names, always used in full. **No
renaming — only qualification.** Resolves `ISSUE-0061`.

**`ADR-0047` — three representations of knowledge.** Semantic (the canonical
graph), Authoring (human-editable sources), Presentation (generated views). **The
compiler maintains semantic equivalence across them.** They are different views
of the same knowledge, not different knowledge.

## What was learned

**Two contradictions dissolved without any ADR being superseded or corrected.**
`ISSUE-0059` and `ISSUE-0061` both looked like conflicts between accepted
decisions. Both turned out to be conflicts in the *reading*: independence was
being read as isolation, and two valid concepts were being read as one scheme.
`ADR-0040`, `ADR-0041`, `ADR-0037` and `ADR-0043` all survive untouched.

That is worth contrasting with the four corrections the project has needed. The
distinguishing question seems to be whether the decision was wrong or the
vocabulary was.

**`ADR-0044` pre-decided a placement before anything needed it.** Inference
Rules do not exist and may never. Fixing *where they would go* removes the
predictable failure: a small rule added to a dimension definition, then another,
until dimensions quietly contain a rules engine. Deciding placement in advance
costs nothing and closes a shortcut.

**The `ISSUE-0060` crux was answered on the horn I judged more likely, and the
reasoning generalizes.** Front matter is a *serialization of a relationship*, not
a property. That rescued `ADR-0017` and `ADR-0042` simultaneously — and
`ADR-0047` then showed it was never a special case: it is one instance of three
representations of one body of knowledge.

**`ADR-0046` is the third application of one discipline.** State names
(`ADR-0025`), normative artifact types (`ADR-0030`), and now classification
schemes. Three ADRs applying the same rule is a rule, and it belongs in a
`ModelingPolicy` rather than being rediscovered a fourth time. Added to M3.

**"Semantic equivalence" is a strong and currently unverifiable claim.**
`ADR-0047` makes the compiler responsible for it, which means a projection that
loses meaning is a *defect* rather than a limitation. Nothing yet defines what
equivalence means operationally — round-trip fidelity, information preservation,
or something weaker — and no compiler exists to test it.

**The same question has now been deferred three times.** The four undefined
dimensions travelled from `ISSUE-0057` to `ISSUE-0059` to `ISSUE-0062`. Each
resolution answered the structural question and postponed the definitional one.
Individually defensible; collectively a pattern. It is recorded as such in
`ISSUE-0062`, because the Dimension Registry cannot be written without it.
