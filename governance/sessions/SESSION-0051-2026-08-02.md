## What was learned

### The tool's most important behaviour is a refusal

Everything else in this repository runs in CI. This one stops.

The reviewer's instruction — *do not attempt to simulate it* — is easy to agree
with and hard to enforce, because a simulated curation session is **three lines
of code** and its output is indistinguishable in the record from a real one.
Every other measurement here was produced by a script, so the archive has no way
to tell them apart after the fact.

**Refusing at the point of execution is the only enforcement that survives its
own author.**

### `correct` is the decision that makes it a partner

A gate offers accept and reject. This offers a third: *right idea, wrong words* —
the proposal is kept with the reviewer's own statement.

**A correction is the most valuable thing a session can produce.** It is human
engineering judgement, captured, in a form the model can hold — and it is
invisible to every metric this project had, because a corrected proposal counts
as an accepted one.

It is also the sharpest signal about the *skill*: a proposal right enough to keep
and wrong as stated separates a partner from a generator.

### The Engineering Review is a curation feature, not a Discovery feature

`ADR-0142` reads as a Discovery decision and is not one. Nothing downstream
consumes a review; it is the first artifact in this repository **whose only
consumer is a person.**

It also gives a non-deterministic producer somewhere honest to put what it cannot
prove. A frontier model reading 135 markdown documents will form impressions that
are useful and not admissible as facts. **As a proposal that is fabrication; as
an argument addressed to a human who may disagree, it is what an expert partner
offers.**

### The five responsibilities were already the architecture

Stated together, they are one design. Each boundary was drawn separately, for a
local reason, over many sessions — and **every significant defect this project
found was found at one of them**, by comparing what two responsibilities said
about the same evidence.

Two acquisition modes disagreeing. A blind worker reading only the extractor's
output. Guidance reading a model it did not build. **None was found by inspecting
a component alone.**

It is also what makes admitting a frontier model safe: it may hypothesize freely
precisely because it cannot extract, cannot authorize and cannot be consumed
directly. **Determinism is preserved by the boundary, not by the worker.**

### The measurement is empty, and empty is the honest value

`453 proposals generated · 0 reviewed`.

Nothing will fill that in until a person sits down with it. Under every previous
framing this session would have shipped a number.
